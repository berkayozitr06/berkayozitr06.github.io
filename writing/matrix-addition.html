Adding two matrices: theory and a Python example

18 August 2023 · Linear algebra

The definition

Let \(A\) and \(B\) be two \(m \times n\) matrices:

$$ A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1} & \cdots & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix}_{m \times n}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} b_{11} & \cdots & b_{1n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ b_{m1} & \cdots & b_{mn} \end{pmatrix}_{m \times n}. $$

Their sum is obtained by adding corresponding entries. In other words, \((A+B)_{ij} = a_{ij} + b_{ij}\) for every \(i = 1, \dots, m\) and \(j = 1, \dots, n\):

$$ A+B = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11}+b_{11} & \cdots & a_{1n}+b_{1n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1}+b_{m1} & \cdots & a_{mn}+b_{mn} \end{pmatrix}_{m \times n}. $$

Note that the sum is only defined when both matrices have the same size.

A Python example

With NumPy, the + operator adds two arrays entry by entry, which is exactly the definition above. 

import numpy as np

# Create two 3x3 matrices
A = np.array([[5, 7, 3],
              [4, 5, 9],
              [4, 2, 1]])

B = np.array([[2, 9, 8],
              [4, 7, 3],
              [5, 8, 6]])

# Add the matrices
result = A + B

print(result)

What the code computes

$$ A = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 7 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 9 \\ 4 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 9 & 8 \\ 4 & 7 & 3 \\ 5 & 8 & 6 \end{bmatrix} $$
$$ A+B = \begin{bmatrix} 5+2 & 7+9 & 3+8 \\ 4+4 & 5+7 & 9+3 \\ 4+5 & 2+8 & 1+6 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 16 & 11 \\ 8 & 12 & 12 \\ 9 & 10 & 7 \end{bmatrix} $$
